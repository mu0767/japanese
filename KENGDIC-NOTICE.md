# Kengdic-derived Korean glossary

`korean-kengdic.js` and `korean-kengdic-provenance.json` contain selected, reviewed entries derived from Kengdic, **Joe Speigle and contributors**.

- Original data and contributor notices: https://github.com/garfieldnate/kengdic/blob/master/kengdic.tsv
- Project and original source availability: https://github.com/garfieldnate/kengdic
- Original license choice: MPL 2.0 or LGPL 2.0 or later. This distribution chooses **MPL 2.0**, retained in [MPL-2.0.txt](MPL-2.0.txt).
- Modifications: selected dictionary entries were linked to OpenJLPT IDs through the English gloss, checked against Japanese spelling and reading, and shortened or inflected into learning-oriented Korean. Per-entry source IDs and original English/Korean records remain in `korean-kengdic-provenance.json`.
- Modified source-form data and regeneration code: https://github.com/mu0767/japanese . Run `node build-korean.cjs --from-snapshot` to regenerate.

These MPL-covered data files remain separate from the CC BY-SA adapted glossary. Existing source links and contributor notices are retained. No warranty is offered. Other dictionary sources and editorial translations are described in KOREAN-NOTICE.md.
